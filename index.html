About Ho Chi Minh City

Ho Chi Minh City is a vibrant metropolis in southern Vietnam, known for its rich history, diverse culture, delicious food, modern architecture, and energetic streets.

From historic landmarks and traditional markets to modern skyscrapers and lively nightlife, the city offers a wide variety of experiences for visitors.

Famous Attractions

Ben Thanh Market

One of the city's most recognizable markets, offering local food, souvenirs, clothing, and traditional products.

War Remnants Museum

A museum that provides insight into the Vietnam War and its impact on the country and its people.

Independence Palace

A historic landmark that played an important role in Vietnam's modern history.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

A famous architectural landmark located in the heart of District 1.

Nguyen Hue Walking Street

A popular pedestrian area surrounded by restaurants, cafes, shops, and city landmarks.

Landmark 81

One of Vietnam's most recognizable modern skyscrapers, offering restaurants, shopping, entertainment, and city views.

68 Bitexco Financial Tower

Bitexco Financial Tower is one of Ho Chi Minh City's most iconic landmarks, offering stunning city views and a symbol of Saigon's modern skyline.

Bui Vien Walking Street

A lively street in District 1 known for its restaurants, cafes, street food, nightlife, and energetic atmosphere.

Must-Try Vietnamese Food

Phở

A Vietnamese noodle soup made with broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat.

Bánh Mì

A crispy Vietnamese sandwich filled with meat, vegetables, herbs, and sauces.

Cơm Tấm

A popular Southern Vietnamese dish made with broken rice, grilled pork, vegetables, and fish sauce.

Bánh Xèo

A crispy Vietnamese savory pancake filled with shrimp, pork, bean sprouts, and herbs.

Chè

A traditional Vietnamese dessert that comes in many varieties with beans, fruit, jelly, and coconut milk.

Vietnamese Iced Coffee

Strong Vietnamese coffee combined with sweetened condensed milk and ice.
